Privacy Policy

TapTrivia — Visudo Labs, LLC

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

Visudo Labs, LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the TapTrivia mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Display Name: The nickname you choose when joining a game. This is visible to other players on the in-game leaderboard.
  • Device Identifier: A unique device identifier used to maintain your game session and saved location preferences. This is generated locally and is not linked to your personal identity.
  • Game Activity: Your trivia answers, scores, streaks, and game participation history to provide gameplay features and leaderboards.
  • Purchase Information: Subscription status is managed through RevenueCat and the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not directly collect or store your payment information (credit card numbers, billing addresses, etc.). All payment processing is handled by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat.
  • Saved Locations: The codes and names of venues you have joined are stored locally on your device to provide quick re-join functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate the trivia game experience
  • Display your nickname on in-game leaderboards
  • Maintain your game session across questions
  • Remember your saved locations for quick re-joining
  • Manage your subscription status
  • Improve the App and fix bugs

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your display name is visible to other players at the same venue during active trivia games via the leaderboard. We share data with the following third-party services:

  • RevenueCat: Manages subscription status and purchase validation. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.
  • Apple / Google: Process in-app subscription payments. See their respective privacy policies.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): Hosts our backend infrastructure. Data is stored in the United States.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your saved locations and display name are stored locally on your device. Game activity and leaderboard data are stored on our servers hosted on Amazon Web Services in the United States. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

Game session data is retained for the duration of active gameplay and leaderboard display. We do not maintain long-term profiles tied to your identity. Locally stored data (saved locations, nickname) persists on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data.

6. Children’s Privacy

TapTrivia is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

You can delete your locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data through your device settings. To request deletion of data associated with your device, email privacy@visudolabs.com with your bar code and approximate play dates.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App or on our website. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
